Jonathan's Boxes

Why Boxes?
If you can build a box you can make many other things: bookshelves, end tables, closet shelves. And you develop the skills for many more things inlcuding tables, and desks. And, after you develop drawer making skills, bureaus. So boxes are a good place to begin.
These are ideas for how to make the most of a small living space.
I built these things for a very special person who lives in an efficiency appartment.
