Remedies for Spiritual Depression

Device #1 SATAN FOCUSES OUR ATTENTION ON OUR SIN AND AWAY FROM JESUS
Device #2 SATAN FILLS OUR MINDS WITH FALSE DEFINITIONS OF GOD'S GRACE
Device #3 SATAN TELLS BELIEVERS GOD DOESN'T LOVE THEM BECAUSE BAD THINGS HAPPENED TO THEM
Device #4 SATAN TELLS BELIEVERS THEIR EXPERIENCE OF GOD'S GRACE IS COUNTERFEIT
Device #5 SATAN TELLS BELIEVERS THEIR STRUGGLES ARE IDENTICAL TO THOSE OF NON-BELIEVERS
Device #6 SATAN TELLS BELIEVERS, "YOU LOST YOUR JOY BECAUSE YOU NEVER HAD IT."
Device #7 SATAN TELLS THE BELIEVER GOD WON'T KEEP FORGIVING YOU BECAUSE YOU HAVEN'T OVERCOME THAT SAME KIND OF SIN
Device #8 SATAN SAYS, "GOD CAN'T LOVE SOMEONE WHO STRUGGLES WITH SIN THE WAY YOU DO"
